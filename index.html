Cybersecurity Awareness

Most breaches begin with a human mistake. These habits stop the majority of everyday attacks.

🎣 Spot phishing

  • Check the real sender address, not just the display name.
  • Hover over links before clicking; look for look-alike domains.
  • Be wary of urgency, threats, or prizes.
  • When in doubt, contact the company through its official channel.

🔑 Passwords & MFA

  • Use long passphrases (14+ characters) and a password manager.
  • Never reuse passwords across accounts.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication, preferably an authenticator app or security key.

📱 Mobile & Wi-Fi safety

  • Install apps only from official stores.
  • Avoid sensitive logins on public Wi-Fi; use mobile data or a trusted VPN.
  • Keep phone and apps updated.

💾 Backups & updates

  • Follow the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offline/offsite.
  • Enable automatic updates for OS, browser and apps.
  • Test restoring backups, not just creating them.

💬 Social engineering

  • Verify unexpected requests for money, codes or access by a second channel.
  • Never share OTP codes, even with someone claiming to be support.
  • Limit what you post publicly about work and personal life.

💸 Mobile money & scams

  • Never send money to claim a prize or loan.
  • Confirm payment requests by calling the person directly.
  • Report fraud to your provider immediately.

Ethical Penetration Testing

Penetration testing is an authorized, scoped simulation of an attack to find weaknesses before criminals do.

⚠ Legal & ethical rule: Only test systems you own or have written permission to test. Unauthorized testing is illegal. Practice in your own lab or on legal training platforms.

The 5 phases

Planning & scoping. Define targets, rules of engagement, testing windows, and sign a written authorization. Agree what is out of bounds and how to handle sensitive data.

Reconnaissance. Gather information about the target from public sources (OSINT): domains, technologies, exposed services, public staff information. Passive recon avoids touching the target directly.

Scanning & enumeration. Identify live hosts, open ports, services and known vulnerabilities, using tools such as Nmap and vulnerability scanners, within the agreed scope.

Exploitation & post-exploitation. Safely validate that weaknesses are real and show business impact, while avoiding damage or downtime. Document every action for traceability.

Reporting & retest. Deliver an executive summary, technical findings ranked by risk, evidence, and clear remediation steps. Retest after fixes to confirm closure.

Frameworks & learning path

Standards to know

  • OWASP Top 10 (web apps)
  • PTES (Penetration Testing Execution Standard)
  • NIST SP 800-115
  • MITRE ATT&CK

Common tools (overview)

  • Nmap: network discovery
  • Wireshark: packet analysis
  • Burp Suite: web testing
  • Metasploit: exploit framework (lab use)

Safe places to practice

  • Your own VirtualBox lab (Kali Linux + intentionally vulnerable VMs)
  • TryHackMe and Hack The Box
  • OWASP Juice Shop and DVWA

Certifications

  • CompTIA Security+ and PenTest+
  • CEH
  • eJPT, then OSCP

Blue Team: Defending the Network

The blue team detects, responds to and prevents attacks. Pen testers show where the holes are; blue teamers close and watch them.

🛡 Harden

  • Patch quickly; remove unused services and accounts.
  • Apply least privilege and network segmentation.
  • Change default credentials; disable insecure protocols.
  • Secure routers and switches: strong admin passwords, SSH not Telnet, updated firmware.

👁 Detect

  • Centralize logs (SIEM such as Wazuh or Security Onion, both free/open source).
  • Monitor failed logins, new admin accounts, unusual traffic.
  • Deploy IDS/IPS such as Suricata or Snort.
  • Use EDR/antivirus with central alerts.

🚨 Respond

  • Have a written incident response plan.
  • Follow: Prepare, Identify, Contain, Eradicate, Recover, Lessons learned.
  • Preserve evidence; keep a timeline.
  • Practice with tabletop exercises.

🎯 Hunt & improve

  • Map detections to MITRE ATT&CK techniques.
  • Proactively hunt for suspicious behavior.
  • Run purple-team exercises with pen testers.
  • Track metrics: time to detect and time to respond.
